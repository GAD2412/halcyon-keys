Port Halcyon, the Keys. Golden hour never lasts.

  • Open world: Port Halcyon with 6 districts, beach boulevard, docks, downtown towers, pier and Halo Wheel
  • On foot and by car: steal any car, arcade drift handling, chase camera, 8 vehicle models
  • Lane traffic with signals, pedestrians who walk the sidewalks and flee from danger
  • 5-star wanted level: PHPD cruisers, interceptors, MANTA-1 helicopter, roadblocks, Busted and Wasted
  • Combat: fists, Tamsin 9 pistol, Hornet SMG, health and armor, tracers and muzzle flashes
  • Story: 9 missions in 3 acts (steal, tail, defend, courier, stakeout, heist, convoy, escape) with dialogue cards and portraits
  • Side activities: 3 street time trials and Halcyon Cabs taxi fares; cash, respect and ranks
  • HUD: rotating minimap with GPS route and police blips, stars, speedometer, mission card
  • Pause menu with city map, story, features, controls, settings and 3 save slots
  • Day-night cycle from golden hour to neon night
  • Synthesised audio: engine, sirens, gunshots, radio station

WASD move · Mouse look · F enter/steal car · Esc pause · M map
