Port Halcyon, the Keys. Golden hour never lasts.

  • Open world: Port Halcyon with 6 districts, beach boulevard, downtown towers, Lantern Night Market, docks, pier and Halo Wheel
  • On foot and by car: steal any car, arcade drift handling, chase camera, 9 vehicle models
  • Lane traffic (cars, vans, cabs, Coastline buses) with signals; pedestrians who walk the sidewalks and flee
  • 5-star wanted level: PHPD cruisers, interceptors, MANTA-1 helicopter, roadblocks, Busted and Wasted
  • Combat: fists, Tamsin 9 pistol, Hornet SMG, health and armor, tracers and muzzle flashes
  • Story: 9 missions in 3 acts (steal, tail, defend, courier, stakeout, heist, convoy, escape) with dialogue cards and portraits
  • Street races: 3 routes against 3 rival drivers, with position, checkpoint and timer on the HUD
  • Halcyon Cabs taxi fares, 8 stunt jumps and 20 lost postcards to find; cash, respect, ranks and a completion tracker
  • HUD: rotating minimap with GPS route and police blips, wanted status, dispatch roster, rank, weapon slots, health, armor, car condition
  • Pause menu with city map, story, stats, features, controls, settings and 3 save slots plus autosave
  • Day-night cycle from golden hour to neon night, night rain with wet roads, live traffic signals
  • Synthesised audio: engine, sirens, gunshots, three radio stations
